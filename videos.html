Digiteka · Generative video

This article is about to become a vertical video. I built the machine that does it.

At Digiteka I designed and led the pipeline that turns a news article into a video made for phones. Keep scrolling. The page will do to this story what the software does to a publisher's.

By Loqmann BenhaddyaArchitect and lead developerReading time: one scroll

Digiteka runs video for news publishers. By 2025 their readers had moved to short vertical formats, and the company was launching a vertical video feed. The feed needed videos. Newsrooms produce articles. My job was to close that gap automatically.

I began with a benchmark, not with code. Five generative video models, tested on real press images and compared on cost, speed and quality. The one I kept is billed per video instead of per second, which made it several times cheaper than anything charged by the second, at the quality we needed.

The pipeline has three stages. One model reads the image and the headline and writes a prompt. A second animates the image. A third scores the result and decides whether it ships, goes to a person, or is thrown away. In its first year it generated more than 16,000 videos for five media clients.
